# ShieldBrain — Keyword Research Report

> Basis: the app description, provided keyword list, competitor landscape knowledge and search-intent
> analysis. No live search-volume tools were queried; volume/competition labels are directional
> estimates — validate in Google Search Console + Keyword Planner after launch.

## 1. Keyword strategy summary

- **Primary keyword (site-wide / home page):** `reels counter` + `shorts blocker`
  (the app's literal category — low competition, exact-match to the Play listing title, high download intent).
- **Core strategy:** win the **long-tail "block X" queries** (high intent, weak competition — mostly
  forum threads and outdated blog posts rank today) and the **problem queries** ("stop doomscrolling"),
  then funnel every page to the Google Play CTA.
- **Moat keyword:** `reels counter` — almost no app markets scroll *counting*. ShieldBrain can own
  this term and its question-forms outright ("how many reels do I watch", "app that counts reels").
- **Install-first prioritisation:** pages target searchers with an Android phone and an active pain,
  not generic "social media addiction" info traffic.

## 3. Search-intent mapping & funnel

```
Awareness    "why can't I stop watching reels"      → doomscrolling guide
Problem      "stop doomscrolling", "reels addiction"→ doomscrolling / dopamine detox guides
Solution     "block instagram reels", "shorts blocker" → platform guides
Measurement  "how many reels do I watch"            → reels-counter guide (unique moat)
Download     "reels blocker app android free"       → home page → Google Play
```

Every guide answers the search intent first (quick-answer lead paragraph), teaches the topic,
demonstrates the ShieldBrain solution mid-page, and ends with a Play Store CTA.

## 4. Internal linking strategy (implemented)

- **Hub-and-spoke:** Home ↔ all guides; `/guides/` hub lists all; footer on every page links all 8 guides.
- **Platform cross-links:** each platform guide links the other three ("habits migrate" narrative gives a natural reason to link).
- **Behaviour chain:** doomscrolling ↔ dopamine detox ↔ reels counter ↔ screen time (each links the next step of the reader's journey).
- **Money links:** every page has ≥2 links to the Play Store (nav CTA + end CTA) and ≥1 link to Home.
- **Anchor text:** descriptive and keyword-bearing ("block Instagram Reels", never "click here").
